Privacy Policy

This is an English translation of the German Datenschutzerklärung provided for convenience. In case of any discrepancy, the German version governs.

Protecting your data matters to us. This privacy policy explains what data is processed when you use the "Fairydoku" app. Our principle: Fairydoku collects as little data as possible. There is no registration and no user account.

1. Controller

The controller responsible for data processing under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) is:

App HUMB UG (haftungsbeschränkt)

Parkstraße 9

31188 Holle, Germany

Email: info@humb.ug

Represented by the Managing Director: Marco Pilipovic

2. Principle of data minimization

Fairydoku is a pure logic game and free to use. We ourselves do not collect any personal data and do not operate our own user management. There is no sign-up. Data processing essentially only occurs through the integrated Google advertising service, described below.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC) is integrated to finance the free App.

There are no banner ads and no ads that appear on their own. Advertising only runs as a video ad that you start yourself, in exchange for a helper or a life.

As long as you don't do that, nothing happens: the ad SDK is not even started, no ad is loaded, and no data is sent to Google. If you never watch a video ad, nothing leaves your device in this context.

The first time you tap an ad button, we ask for your consent beforehand. For this, Google's certified consent tool (User Messaging Platform) is integrated. Only after consent is given does the ad SDK start and load an ad. Without consent, no ad is requested; the App remains fully playable, only the option to watch a video for a reward is unavailable.

During ad delivery, Google may process device and usage information as well as an advertising identifier (Advertising ID) to deliver the ad and prevent abuse (e.g. click fraud).

Every ad request is explicitly marked as non-personalized, and the maximum content rating is set to "G". No interest-based advertising takes place, no user profiles are built, and ads with gambling, violent, or sexual content are excluded.

The legal basis is your consent — for storing information on your device and accessing it, under § 25 (1) TDDDG (the German Telecommunications-Digital-Services Data Protection Act), and for the subsequent processing of your data under Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR. You can change or withdraw it at any time with effect for the future — see section 10. More information can be found in Google's privacy policy.

4. Save data, daily score, and best results

Your score, your daily score, and your past best results are stored locally on your device. No transmission to us takes place, and there is currently no cross-device or shared leaderboard with other players.

We do not ask your permission for this storage, and there is a reason: it is strictly necessary for the App to do what you opened it for — without a saved game, every level would start over from zero. § 25 (2) no. 2 TDDDG exempts exactly this case from the consent requirement. Anything beyond that, we do ask for (see section 3).

The daily score stores how many points were collected during the current day, the best daily result, and the time of the last daily reset. A display name and an avatar fairy can be set in the settings; both are likewise stored only locally. The display name appears in your own daily score on this device; it is not transmitted to us or to other players.

One exception we want to state openly: Android backs up app data to your own Google account on request ("Auto backup"), and Fairydoku participates in this. This lets you find your save again on a new phone. This backup lives in your account, not with us — we have no access to it. You can turn it off in the Android settings under "Backup" or "Google — Backup".

5. Technical provision

Operating the App may involve technically necessary information (e.g. device information), to the extent required for delivery and operation. We do not use any analytics, tracking, or crash-reporting services.

6. Target audience and information for parents

The App's content is non-violent and suitable for any age. As an offering, Fairydoku is directed at people aged 13 and up; it is not an offering directed at children within the meaning of the Play Store Families Policy, and the App does not participate in the "Designed for Families" program.

Regardless of this, no one is served personalized advertising, and no profiles are built. Parents and legal guardians can reach out to the contact address above at any time with questions.

7. Recipients and data transfers to third countries

The recipient of the data described above is Google. This may involve transferring data to countries outside the EU/EEA (in particular the USA). Google bases such transfers on appropriate safeguards (e.g. EU Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework).

8. Retention period

We ourselves do not store any personal data. The retention period for data processed by Google is governed by its own privacy policy.

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the following rights: access to processed data (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification of inaccurate data (Art. 16 GDPR), erasure (Art. 17 GDPR), restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR), data portability (Art. 20 GDPR), and objection to processing (Art. 21 GDPR).

A message to the contact address above is sufficient to exercise these rights. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

10. Changing or withdrawing consent

You can change your advertising choice at any time in the App's settings under "Change privacy settings". This option appears there once consent has been requested. If you withdraw it, no more ads will be delivered from that point on.

If you wish to object to processing beyond this, please contact the address above.

Independently of this, you can delete or reset your device's advertising identifier at any time yourself: in the Android settings under "Privacy" or "Google", in the "Ads" section. Apps will no longer receive an advertising identifier afterward.

11. Changes to this privacy policy

We will update this privacy policy when changes to the App or the legal situation make it necessary. The version linked in the App or on the Play Store at the time applies.

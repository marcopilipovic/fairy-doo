## Full description (max. 4000 characters)

```
You are the warden of a fairy realm. Place fairies on a mossy forest grid
without their magic getting in each other's way — a calm logic puzzle for in
between, growing denser with every level.

THE RULES

• exactly one fairy per row
• exactly one fairy per column
• exactly one fairy per glade — those are the glowing coloured areas
• no two fairies touch, not even diagonally

There is nothing else to learn. The tutorial shows it in two screens, and
after that you can play.

HOW YOU PLAY

A short tap places an X — your note saying "no fairy lives here". Press and
hold to call the fairy. While solving you rule out far more squares than you
place fairies, which is why the marker sits on the quicker gesture.

THE FOREST GROWS DENSER

The first grid is 4×4. Every two levels it gains a row and a column, until
from level 9 an 8×8 grid with eight fairies lies before you. A different
fairy lives in each glade, and across ten levels every one of them passes
through every glade — ten characters, each with her own name and colour.

WHEN YOU GET STUCK

Fairy dust places a fairy on a square that is guaranteed correct. The
will-o'-the-wisp does the opposite and reveals a square where none belongs.
The fairy ring is the third helper: for half a minute, every fairy you place
marks the squares she rules out herself — it saves you the tapping, not the
thinking.

All three grow back on their own. Nothing has to be bought, nothing has to be
unlocked.

You have three attempts per level. One is only spent when you place a fairy
where she clashes — removing and marking are free. Once all three are gone,
the level is lost.

No clock is running. You can think as long as you like, get up, come back —
the puzzle waits.

Losing a level costs one of five forest lives. One grows back every two
hours; anyone who does not want to wait can watch a short video by choice.

THE FAIRY PATH

Your levels lie as a glowing path through a night forest. Points, lives and
the daily score stand above it — under your name, if you like. That is the
reason to come back tomorrow.

SOUND

A calm pad lies over the whole forest — it keeps playing as you move between
puzzle and map. When a fairy sits right, she giggles; six different ones take
turns. Everything else sounds muted and brief so it does not disturb your
thinking. Only when you finish a level does the forest music step aside and
let the cheer through.

Music, sounds and fairy voices are adjusted separately — with the slider or a
tap on the speaker beside it.

FAIR, NO CATCHES

• free, no in-app purchases
• no banner ads, no ads that appear by themselves
• ads only when you start a video yourself to get a helper
• no sign-up, no account
• we collect nothing about you — your progress stays on your device and, if
  you use Android's backup, in your own Google account
• playable offline

Fairydoku is aimed at people aged 13 and over and is not directed at
children. The content itself is non-violent and harmless at any age — the age
limit is down to the advertising, not the game.
```

## Reviewer instructions

**Where:** App content → "App access".

The app needs no credentials, so **"All functionality is available without
special access"** is the correct choice. For anyone who wants to write
something anyway:

```
The app requires no sign-in and no credentials. All functionality is
available immediately.

Advertising appears exclusively as an optional rewarded video which the
player starts themselves in order to receive an in-game helper. There are no
banners and no ads that appear on their own. Google's consent tool (UMP) runs
before the first ad.

The ad buttons appear from level four onwards; before that a gift takes their
place.
```
